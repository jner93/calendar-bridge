# Privacy Policy — Calendar Bridge

Last updated: October 9, 2026

## What this app does

Calendar Bridge is a personal-use tool that synchronizes calendar events
between the owner's iCloud calendar and Google Calendar. It runs on the
owner's own Mac mini and serves only the owner.

## Data collection and use

- The app reads calendar events (titles, times, locations, attendees) from
  the owner's iCloud calendar and Google Calendar, solely to keep the two
  in sync.
- Data is transmitted directly between the owner's devices and Apple/Google
  servers. No calendar data is sent to any third party, sold, or used for
  advertising.
- No analytics, tracking, or telemetry of any kind.

## Data storage

- Google OAuth tokens and an iCloud app-specific password are stored locally
  on the owner's Mac mini with restricted file permissions.
- Sync state (event ID mappings) is stored locally on the same machine.

## Data retention and deletion

- Calendar data is retained only as long as needed for synchronization.
- Deleting the app and its local files removes all stored credentials and state.
